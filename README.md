*"I thought I'd be okay with it, but it hurts. Keep this a secret, okay?" / "Why couldn't you keep it a secret from me too?"* - Lim Joo-kyung

*True Beauty* is a south korean TV series directed by Kim Sang-hyeop and written by Lee Si-eun. True Beauty comes from self acceptance and inner worth rather than superfacial appearances or societal standards

## 💟Why I Love it
* * The Cozy, Comforting Nostalgia:* The self-acceptance, authenticity and the emotional resonance.
* * The Characters:* Moon Ga-young, Cha Eun-woo, Hwang In-youp and Park Yoo-na.
* * The Upside Down:* The Aftermath, she faces intense pressure and cruelty from her peers, causing her world to unravel as people turn on her.

*Falling Into Your Smile* is a chinese romantic, comedy and esport drama was directed by Qiu Zhongwei (also credited as David Q). The series blends live-action team life with animated gaming sequences detailing match mechanics and strategies.

## 💟Why I Love it
* * The Cozy, Comforting Nostalgia:* The youthful ambition, first love and the early gaming memories.
* * The Characters:*  Cheng Xiao, Xu Kai, Lao K, Xiao Pang,  Lao Mao, Lu Yue and  Yu Ming.
* * The Upside Down:* The Career-Ending Injuries, the characters face dangerous real-life harassment, including a disturbing incident where an obsessed fan sends a razor blade hidden in a package to injure Tong Yao.

*"You know what's different about a soulmate? I can see myself in that person's eyes and I look so, so happy."* - Do Bong-soon
*Strong Girl Bong-soon* a young woman born with hereditary, superhuman strength passed down through the women in her family

## 💟Why I Love it
* * The Cozy, Comforting Nostalgia:* The Classic Hallyu Comfort, Retro Gaming & Visuals and Iconic OST & Aesthetic.
* * The Characters:* Park Bo-young, Park Hyung-sik and Ji Soo 
* * The Upside Down:* The Flipped Gender Dynamics, The series turns traditional romance tropes upside down by making the tiny female lead the fearless physical protector and the male CEO the dramatic, clingy "damsel" who hides behind her.
